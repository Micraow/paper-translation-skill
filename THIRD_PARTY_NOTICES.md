## README showcase

The preview images in [`docs/images/`](docs/images/) are *page screenshots*, taken from a Chinese typesetting example based on:

> Wanchun Jiang, Haoyang Li, Kai Wang, Yujie Hu, Xiao Han, Danfeng Shan, Fengyuan Ren, Jiawei Huang, and Jianxin Wang. **“CCC: Re-architecting Delay-based Congestion Control in Datacenter Networks.”** *23rd USENIX Symposium on Networked Systems Design and Implementation (NSDI '26)*. [Original USENIX presentation and publication](https://www.usenix.org/conference/nsdi26/presentation/jiang).

The screenshot pages show parts of the paper, including original graphic/algorithm or experimental data excerpts. Their inclusion is **solely illustrative of the workflow** and is not an assertion that these paper excerpts are MIT-licensed. Original authors and/or rights holders retain their rights. The screenshots were edited only to conceal the previous translator's identity in the page footer; underlying scientific content was not retouched. Before redistributing, ensure your use of these specific images is consistent with the paper's applicable terms, permissions and local law; remove them if required.

The **original PDF**, a complete translation of that paper, and its extracted vector graphics are **not** distributed by this repository.

## Fonts

This repository distributes *only font-selection rules*, not any actual font software. XeLaTeX uses fonts installed on the user's computer. Individual fonts have their own licenses.

## Python and LaTeX dependencies

Python libraries such as PyMuPDF and Pillow, XeLaTeX, TeX Live/LaTeX packages and their transitive dependencies are **separate third-party projects with their own licenses**. In particular, evaluate PyMuPDF's AGPL/commercial licensing before distributing an application or service based on it; the project's MIT license does not override those terms. See official project licenses for current details.
